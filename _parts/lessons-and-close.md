Parts 1 and 2 built and operated a working mesh; this part steps back from the
mechanics to ask what goes wrong when teams attempt this pattern. The
anti-patterns chapter draws on the data-mesh literature for the conceptual and
organizational failure modes that derail efforts even when the technology is sound,
and the closing summary reorganizes everything built so far by principle rather than
by concern — for each of Dehghani's four principles, the value it delivers, the
pieces of this project that implement it, and what breaks without it. Part 4 then turns
specifically to what Quarkus itself brought to the build.
