Parts 0 through 4 built, operated, and toured a working data mesh on Quarkus.
This closing part turns the lens on the build itself: the Plan/Execute/Validate
model relay that wrote the code, the fan-out parallelism across file-disjoint
modules that made a multi-hour build survivable, and the adversarial validation
pass that treated every executor's self-report as a claim to check rather than
a result to trust — including the real defects that check actually caught.
