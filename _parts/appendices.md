These six appendices go deeper than the numbered chapters. A1 scales the
WebSockets.Next push implementation with Kafka behind it, including replica
failure and client reconnect. A2 catalogs the gotchas hit while building this
project. A3 gives recommendations for agentic development on this stack. A4
covers the testing pyramid and the test runner in detail. A5 compares Vert.x
in-memory messaging with Kafka. A6 compares the three orchestration engines
point by point. None of them is required reading; pick the one that answers
your question.
